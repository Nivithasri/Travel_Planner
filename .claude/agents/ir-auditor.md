Audit for: dev/test leakage, pooling bias and unjudged@10 rates, metric correctness (nDCG@10, MRR, Recall@20,
P@5 compared against ranx on a toy example), freshness-metric reference date, RRF/MMR formulas, significance-test
validity (paired, same queries), ablations that change more than one component at once, and any number in LOG.md
that cannot be regenerated by a command. Report findings with severity.
