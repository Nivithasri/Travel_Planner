# Phase 9 scoring rubric (D124). Written before any scoring.

Score each itinerary 1 to 5 on four dimensions, from the text and the sources shown on the sheet only.
Use whole numbers. If torn between two scores, take the lower one. Do not look up facts outside the
sheet except to open the link of a live item.

## Feasibility (could a traveller actually do this?)
- 5: days are realistic in length, the order of places makes sense, travel between places is plausible, nothing impossible or contradictory.
- 3: mostly workable but one day is overloaded, a leg looks too long, or a stated limit (days, no night travel, family pace) is stretched.
- 1: impossible or contradictory (places days apart on the same day, ignores the stated trip length or constraints).

## Groundedness (are the claims backed by the sources?)
- 5: nearly every factual statement carries a source marker and the cited text supports it.
- 3: many statements are backed, some are uncited or only loosely supported.
- 1: mostly unsupported or invented specifics (hours, prices, distances) with no sources. An output with no sources at all scores 1 (a property of that system, D124).

## Freshness (are current-status claims supported by something current?)
The corpus has no item dated inside the last 18 months, so corpus citations are never evidence of current status.
- 5: every opening hour, price, closure or running-status claim is supported by a live item with a fetch date, or the answer clearly says it cannot confirm current status.
- 3: some current-status claims are supported or flagged, others are stated as fact without support.
- 1: states current hours, prices or closures as fact with no live support and no caveat.
- A request with no current-status question scores on whether the answer avoids claiming current facts it cannot support.

## Personalization (does it fit what was asked?)
- 5: follows the stated days, budget, party, month, start city, interests and constraints, and answers the question asked.
- 3: fits some of them, ignores one or two.
- 1: generic, ignores most of the request.

Notes column: one short line for any score of 1 or 2 (what was wrong). Do not write the name of a system.
