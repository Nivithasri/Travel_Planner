## Draft permission request: Tamil Nadu Tourism
Status: DRAFT, not sent. To be sent by the project owner from their own address. Replace the bracketed
fields before sending. Nothing from www.tamilnadutourism.tn.gov.in has been collected.

**To:** [Tamil Nadu Tourism contact address, taken from www.tamilnadutourism.tn.gov.in]
**Subject:** Request for permission: non-commercial university research use of www.tamilnadutourism.tn.gov.in

Dear Sir or Madam,

We are two students working on a university Information Retrieval project, a travel-planning system that
answers questions about South India (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and
Puducherry) and cites the sources it used. It is non-commercial, runs locally, and is not published as a
service.

Your terms of use (https://www.tamilnadutourism.tn.gov.in/terms-of-use) say the content is for informational, non-commercial or personal use and may not be copied or posted. We read that as requiring permission for our use, so we are asking before collecting anything.

We would like your written permission to do the following, and nothing more:

1. Read a small number of pages about the destinations listed below, with a polite automated client
   (one request per second at most, a few pages per destination, identified by the User-Agent
   "TravelPlannerResearch/0.1" plus our contact address, honouring your robots.txt).
2. Keep a private local copy of the page text for retrieval experiments in the project.
3. Quote short passages, with the page title and URL as the source, inside generated itineraries.

What we will not do: redistribute your content, publish a copy of it, use it commercially, or fetch
anything outside the pages above. If you prefer a different arrangement (for example a data feed, a list
of pages, or only attribution-based quoting), we will follow it. If you do not want your site used, a
one-line reply is enough and we will delete anything already stored.

Destinations: Chennai, Mahabalipuram, Madurai, Rameswaram, Kanniyakumari, Ooty, Kodaikanal.

Thank you for considering this. We are glad to share a short description of the project, or the list of
exact pages we would read, before you decide.

Yours faithfully,
